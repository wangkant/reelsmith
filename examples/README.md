# Examples

- `workspace/`: the starter. A landscape canvas (1920×1080), English (`en`), no owner rules, the provider settings
  to adjust, empty notes and one sample component (`PriceTag`). Copy it, add a `.env` with your keys and make
  projects in its `projects/`.
- `news-explainer-zh/`: one owner's setup among many: a Chinese (`zh-CN`) news-explainer channel on the `douyin`
  profile (1080×1920, 20-90 s) with a Mandarin voice, hard rules for titles and sources in `rules.json` and a house
  style in its notes. It shows what a workspace can pin down.
- `sea/`: the project of the film at the top of the main README, as the agent left it: `project.json` (a narrated
  landscape explainer: footage clips, a title, an evidence card, two components, a sound bed and captions from the
  voice), `script.md` (its source, lines, picture prompts and storyboard) and the narration's line times. The
  pictures, clips and narration themselves are not in the repository; `speak`, `draw`, `animate` and `capture` make
  them again from `script.md`.
