# @reelsmith/kit

Everything a component may import from `@reelsmith/kit`. **To start, read the Exports block below and the one
section your film needs** ("A component" for a single component, "Drawn-pose animation" for a film's picture,
"Stage kit" for drawn worlds, "Photograph components" for the photo reference); the block
gives every export's props or arguments, defaults and return type, so there is no need to read the sources. The
source is `renderer/src/kit/`; the presets in `renderer/src/presets/` are fuller examples (`library(p, "Receipt")`
prints a preset's source path).

## Exports

Generated from the sources. `<Name ...>` is a component and its props (`name = default`, `name?` optional, no type
means a number); `name(args) → type` a function; `Asset` is an `*_asset` prop as it arrives or a URL with its size.
Every animated piece also takes `f` (the frame; default the current one) and most take a `seed` string. Left out:
`softEllipse`, `softRing`, `hazeBand`, `swayAngle`, `lightMatrix`, `wipeBand`, `quadMatrix` and `PictureContext`,
exported for the kit's own pieces.

## A component

`<workspace>/library/components/Name.tsx` (default export) plus `Name.meta.json` (`name`, a one-line `description`,
`props` as a JSON Schema, an `example`). Every project can use it; a library component shadows a preset of the same
name. Check it with `typecheck(p)`, then `preview(p, [...])` a moment where it shows.

- **Imports allowed**: `react`, `remotion`, `@remotion/*` packages that are installed (`@remotion/paths`,
  `@remotion/layout-utils`, `@remotion/media`), `@reelsmith/kit`, packages the workspace added with `add_package`,
  and files in the same folder or in `library/made`. Anything else (node
  builtins, other packages, absolute paths) is refused when the film is bundled.
- **Where it draws**: inside an absolutely positioned div the size of the clip box, `w × h` from `useClip()`. Lay
  yourself out in that box; text outside it is reported. Clip keyframes (`x`, `scale`, `opacity`...) move the box from
  outside; keyframed `w`/`h` reach you as the current `w`/`h`.
- **Time**: animate from `useCurrentFrame()` (frames from the clip's start) only. No `Date`, no `Math.random`: use
  remotion's `random(seed)` for noise. Every frame is rendered on its own, in any order.
- **Text**: draw it as HTML (`div`/`span`), so the layout check can measure it; size it to the box with `fit` and
  aim for 40 px or more at a 1080 px short side, `Math.min(width, height) / 1080` times that on other canvases
  (the layout check warns under 28 px and render refuses under 20 px, scaled the same way). Use
  `whiteSpace: "pre"` for text you have fitted, so the browser does not wrap it again.
- **SVG ids** (`clipPath`, `mask`, gradients) are global to the page: two copies of a component on screen share them
  unless each instance makes its own. Use React's `useId()`:
  `const gid = "g" + useId().replace(/[^A-Za-z0-9_-]/g, "")`, then `id={gid}` and `url(#${gid})`.
- **Errors**: a component that throws fails `render`; `preview` draws a red box in its place and reports the error.

## How props arrive

Props are exactly what the clip's `props` say in project.json (meta.json defaults are not filled in: use the `*Or`
helpers with your default), except two kinds of key, at any depth (arrays and nested objects too):

| key | in project.json | in the component |
|---|---|---|
| `at`, `*_at` | a Time: `"line:8"`, `"line:8+0.4"`, `"clip:x.end-1"`, `"end"` (film times), or a number = **seconds from the clip's start** | a number: **frames from the clip's start** (negative = before it). A Time that cannot be read arrives unchanged (a string), so `frameOr(props.at, 0)` falls back |
| `asset`, `*_asset` | an asset id | `{src, kind, size?, box?, site?, duration?, animated?}`; an unknown id arrives as the string |

The asset ref (`AssetRef`): `src` a project-relative path (give it to `assetSrc` or `Media`), `size` `[w, h]` px
of an image, `box` the proving sentence in capture px (evidence; phone captures 3× the page, desktop 2×), `duration`
in seconds, `animated` for a GIF, APNG or WebP that moves (`Media` plays it; an `<Img>` holds its first frame).

## useClip()

`const { w, h, dur, fps, theme, width, height, safe, locale, progress, lines } = useClip();`

`w`, `h` the clip box now (keyframed `w`/`h` included); `dur` its length in frames; `fps` the profile's (30 unless
it says otherwise); `theme` the theme's colours; `width`, `height` the canvas; `safe` the safe area `{x, y, w, h}` in
canvas px; `locale` the project's BCP-47 locale (pick words a component adds itself by it); `progress` 0..1 (the
clip's `progress` keyframes, else its elapsed fraction); `lines` the voice lines heard during the clip.

`lines` lets a component time itself to the narration: each line overlapping the clip arrives as `{i, text, from,
to}` (`i` as in `line:N`, `from`/`to` in frames from the clip's start; negative or past `dur` when it began earlier or
runs on). They come from `voice/voice.json`, so a re-recorded voice moves every cue with it.

Theme tokens: `paper` light ground, `ink` text on paper, `night` dark ground, `snow` text on night, `accent` the one
number that matters, `gold` highlights, `good` / `bad` gains and losses, `muted` secondary text. The default theme
`paper` is `#F3EBDD #1E1A16 #16130F #F7F1E6 #C8372D #D9A441 #2E8B57 #C8372D #7A6F62` in that order. Props that take a
colour usually accept a token name or CSS: resolve them with `themeColor`.

## Helpers

What the signatures above do not say:

- **Timing**: `ease` maps frames `a..b` to `from..to` on an ease-out curve, held at both ends (`b <= a`: a step at
  `a`); `springAt` is a 0 → 1 spring from frame `at` (0 before), damping 200 = no overshoot
  (`springAt(f, at, { fps, damping: 12 })`); `clamp` is `interpolate` options that hold both ends; `toFrames` rounds;
  `spread` gives `n` frames evenly from `a` to `b` (one item: `[a]`).
- **Props**: `numOr` a finite number or the fallback; `frameOr` the same for a Time prop (frames from the clip
  start); `isAsset` true for an asset ref; `assetSrc` the URL for `<Img>` / `<OffthreadVideo>`.
- **Text**: `FONTS` `sans` (Noto Sans SC, 100–900: body, numbers), `serif` (Noto Serif SC, 200–900: quotes),
  `display` (Smiley Sans, one oblique weight: headlines), `zcool` (ZCOOL QingKe HuangYou, one loud weight).
  `fontFamily` turns a `FONTS` key or a workspace font (`library/fonts`) into a CSS family. `fit` is the largest size
  ≤ `max` at which `text` fits `width` on one line; `fitLines` one line, or two when one would be under `min`;
  `fitBlock` wraps into up to `maxLines` at the largest size ≥ `min` (use it for text that must stay above the
  readable floor). Render their `lines.join("\n")` with `whiteSpace: "pre"`. `twoLines` and `breakLines` split only
  where text may break (never inside a word, a number and its unit, or before closing punctuation; `han` lets Han
  text break between characters); `words` segments with Intl.Segmenter; `textWidth(..., true)` measures tabular
  digits; `fmt(2700.5, 1)` → `"2,700.5"`; `decimalsOf(8.55)` → `2`. A number that counts: lay it out once at the
  width of its final text with `fontVariantNumeric: "tabular-nums"` (see the `CountUp` preset).
- **Colour**: `themeColor` turns a token name into its colour, other strings pass as CSS, empty gives the
  fallback token; `onGround` gives text colours `{dark, ink, muted, faint}` for a `"light"` or `"dark"` ground.

### Pictures

- `Media`: an image or video asset filling its parent, `fit` cover/contain around a `focus`, an optional slow `motion` (`push`, `pull`, `pan_left`, `pan_right`, `rise`, `still`), `dim` 0..1 veil; an animated image ref plays, looping; video: `trimIn` (frames), `rate`, `loop`, `volume`, `volumeKeys` (a volume curve, frames from the clip start), `fadeIn`/`fadeOut` (frames), `hold` (a source frame shown throughout) or `rateKeys` (a speed curve), both silent (`<Media media={props.photo_asset} motion="push" dim={0.3} />`)
- `EvidenceCrop`: the evidence clip's own crop in a `w × h` window: the proving sentence scaled to the window's width (at most 2×) with up to `context` capture px of page above and below, in a panel that hugs that content (centred); where it cuts into the page it fades into `background` (white); optional `highlight` `{at, dur (frames), color}` sweep
- `evidenceLayout`: where `EvidenceCrop` puts things, or null: `s` (capture px → window px), `mark` (the sentence), `panel` (the part of the window the content fills), `fade`, all in window px (`const { panel } = evidenceLayout(ev, w, 400, 120)!`)

`ClipContext` is the React context behind `useClip()`, for test harnesses.

## A complete component

A ring that fills to `pct` at `at` with the percentage counting inside and a label under it. It shows a Time prop,
fitted text, theme colours on either ground, and a per-instance SVG id.

```tsx
// library/components/RingStat.tsx: a ring that fills to `pct` from `at`, the percentage counting up inside it.
import { useId } from "react";
import { useCurrentFrame } from "remotion";
import { ease, fit, fmt, FONTS, frameOr, numOr, onGround, themeColor, useClip } from "@reelsmith/kit";

export default function RingStat({ props }: { props: any }) {
  const { w, h, fps, theme } = useClip();
  const f = useCurrentFrame(); // frames from the clip's start
  const gid = "ring" + useId().replace(/[^A-Za-z0-9_-]/g, ""); // unique per instance: two rings never share an id
  const at = frameOr(props.at, 0); // a Time prop arrives as frames from the clip's start
  if (f < at) return null; // nothing before its moment
  const pct = Math.max(0, Math.min(100, numOr(props.pct, 0)));
  const t = ease(f, at, at + Math.round(1.2 * fps)); // 0 -> 1 over 1.2 s, then held
  const ink = onGround(theme, props.ground);
  const color = themeColor(theme, props.color, "accent");
  const label = String(props.label ?? "");
  const labelSize = label ? fit(label, w * 0.9, Math.min(64, h * 0.12), FONTS.sans, 800) : 0;
  const d = Math.min(w, h - labelSize * 1.6) * 0.92; // the ring, with room left for the label
  const sw = d * 0.12;
  const r = (d - sw) / 2;
  const len = 2 * Math.PI * r;
  const numSize = fit(`${fmt(pct)}%`, r * 1.3, r * 0.7, FONTS.sans, 900); // sized at the final text
  return (
    <div style={{ position: "absolute", inset: 0, display: "flex", flexDirection: "column", alignItems: "center", justifyContent: "center" }}>
      <div style={{ position: "relative", width: d, height: d }}>
        <svg width={d} height={d} style={{ position: "absolute", inset: 0 }}>
          <defs>
            <linearGradient id={gid} x1="0" y1="0" x2="1" y2="1">
              <stop offset="0" stopColor={color} />
              <stop offset="1" stopColor={theme.gold} />
            </linearGradient>
          </defs>
          <circle cx={d / 2} cy={d / 2} r={r} fill="none" stroke={ink.faint} strokeWidth={sw} />
          <circle cx={d / 2} cy={d / 2} r={r} fill="none" stroke={`url(#${gid})`} strokeWidth={sw} strokeLinecap="round"
            strokeDasharray={len} strokeDashoffset={len * (1 - (pct / 100) * t)} transform={`rotate(-90 ${d / 2} ${d / 2})`} />
        </svg>
        <div style={{
          position: "absolute", inset: 0, display: "flex", alignItems: "center", justifyContent: "center", fontFamily: FONTS.sans,
          fontWeight: 900, fontSize: numSize, color: ink.ink, fontVariantNumeric: "tabular-nums", whiteSpace: "pre",
        }}>{`${fmt(pct * t)}%`}</div>
      </div>
      {label ? (
        <div style={{ fontFamily: FONTS.sans, fontWeight: 800, fontSize: labelSize, color: ink.ink, marginTop: labelSize * 0.4, whiteSpace: "pre" }}>{label}</div>
      ) : null}
    </div>
  );
}
```

## Stage kit

The stage kit (`renderer/src/kit/stage/`, exported from `@reelsmith/kit` by name) is for performance films: films in
which every narration line is acted out in code instead of laid over a still or a video. The picture is drawn
animation: a character drawn in key poses on a set drawn in the same style ("Drawn-pose animation", next).
`examples/performance/` is a complete film; the manual's "Performance films" section gives the track layout.

### The form

1. The picture is **drawn-pose animation**: a character model sheet, key poses with their twins played by
   `Performer`, a set in layers in the same style, cut-out props with weight, effects drawn in the same line, and
   hard cuts between framings on words. Check it on a 6-a-second and a 25-a-second sheet of the draft.
2. A film is one or more **worlds**: a component clip that draws a set and performs on it. A world may span the
   whole film and read every narration line from `useClip().lines` (`T("word")` finds a word in any of them), or one
   world may play under each line (`"start": "line:N"`). A head text clip holds the take for at most 4.5 s; captions
   come from the captions track, never from a component. The older layout, one `ground` clip under transparent
   per-line scenes, still works.
3. No image or video clip fills the frame. Pictures enter a world as `*_asset` props: drawn layers and cut-outs in
   `Photo`, or a torn `Cut`.
4. Cues come from the narration: `const { T } = useCues()`, then `T("word")`. Never type a time in a scene.
5. Something lands at least every 1.2 to 2 s, on the word that names it; start the move 2 to 6 frames before `T`.
6. Paper and cut-outs move at 12 poses a second (`step`, `Actor`); ink, mist, swings and falls move on every frame.
7. Words on screen belong to objects (`Label`, `Tag`, `Stamp`, `Card`, `Sign`): 64 px or more, the film's key
   number 140 px or more, one new word per beat.
8. Lay out in the clip's own pixels (`useClip()` `w`, `h`); the hero object spans most of the safe box; keep text
   out of the caption band.
9. Moving or warping one picture is not motion: a cut-out slid across a still, a slow push over a plate, a depth
   bake (`parallax`) or interpolated frames (`tween`) read as a warp when watched.

### Drawn-pose animation

This is how a film's picture is made. A drawn character (a channel's fixed host, or a cast chosen for the one
film) acts as limited TV animation does: each key pose is its own drawing; the `Performer` holds one, crouches and leans against the coming move, snaps to the next, overshoots
and settles, breathes while it holds, flaps its mouth on the voice and blinks. It never pretends to be footage and
needs no photographs, depth, `tween` or `parallax`.

1. **One model sheet** fixes the design. **Every key pose** is a cut-out drawn with `style_ref_asset` = the sheet,
   head to feet, the same outfit named in every prompt; one pose per attitude (explain, doubt, shock, smug, shrug, think).
   draw's report gives each cut-out's `feet` ("It stands at feet 0.52"): copy it into the pose. Read `stock(p)` first:
   kept poses, sets and props cost nothing to take, and `take` with `cast` draws a film's own character its sheet
   and catalogue poses ("Stock", below). Two or three characters each need their own sheet and their own poses.
2. **Twins**, each registered onto its pose's canvas: `draw(p, "pA_shut", "the mouth is closed in a small smile",
   twin_of="pA")`; for blinks `draw(p, "pA_blink", "the eyes are shut", twin_of="pA_shut")` (eyes shut, mouth
   shut) and, optionally, `twin_of="pA"` for `blinkOpen` (eyes shut, mouth open). A third mouth, optional:
   `draw(p, "pA_half", "the mouth is slightly open", twin_of="pA")` as the pose's `half`. Say "mouth" or "eyes" in the
   prompt: only that part is taken from the redraw, whole and with a soft edge, lined up on its surroundings; fur,
   sleeves and outline that the redraw wobbled stay the base's, so nothing flickers when the two swap. The report
   gives the patched box, how far down the figure it sits, and the regions left as the base: a box that sits low
   for a face is the wrong part, draw it again. A report that says NOT A TWIN (nothing changed like a mouth or an
   eye, or the face around it is another drawing) is another pose: draw it again, or use it as a pose of its own
   with its own twins. Look at each twin beside its pose once, enlarged at the mouth. The Performer refuses a twin
   whose pixel size differs from its pose's picture (it would make the figure jump on every mouth flap).
   `usePoses([{ id: "pA", feet: 0.52, twins: ["shut", "half", "blink"] }, { id: "pB", scale: 0.9 }], pics)` builds
   `poses` from the props `pA_asset`, `pA_shut_asset`, `pA_half_asset`, `pA_blink_asset`, `pA_blink_open_asset`; a
   twin listed in `twins` that is missing throws, naming the prop; without `twins` it takes whichever are present.
3. **Preview a PoseSheet before staging**: `<PoseSheet poses={poses} h={520} />` as one still shows every pose on
   one ground line as the Performer places it (pick `h` so they fit side by side), a tick at each `feet` and a guide
   at the first pose's head top: even out head size with `scale` and the stance with `feet` until both hold.
   Poses stand on their soles: the clear rows under a cut-out are measured from the picture, so `y` is where the
   soles touch (for a plain string `src`, give `inset: [top, bottom]` in image px). `show="shut"` (or `"half"`,
   `"blink"`) shows a twin of each pose instead.
4. **The set** as drawn layers in the same style; **props** as cut-outs in `Photo` (`anchor` is the pivot, `r` turns
   about it, `w` × sx with `sy` = sy / sx squashes), moved by `drop`, `toss` (an arc that lands and squashes), `swing`.
5. **Beats** from cue times, **effects** under one `LineStyle` in the drawings' line colour, **cuts** between
   framings with `Framing` (wide, mid, a close shot of the detail): hard cuts on words, never camera stations or a
   slow move over one picture.

**Pose sizes**: `h` is the drawn height of a pose's canvas at scale 1, in clip px; `feet` is the x of the point
midway between the feet as a fraction of the canvas width (default 0.5; take it from the draw report); `scale`
multiplies one pose so its head matches the others. The Performer stands the canvas's bottom edge on `y` with the
`feet` point on `x`. **Mouth and eyes**: while the voice is heard the open-mouth `src` shows (a blink uses
`blinkOpen` if given, otherwise it is skipped); in silence `shut`, and `blink` while blinking. A pose with `half`
has three mouths, picked by loudness on twos: shut, half (above `open`), open (above `wide`, staying open while
above `narrow`); a syllable opens through half and every shape holds 2 frames or more; no blink shows during half.
`mouthAt` gives the mouth (0, 1, 2) at a frame. Beats may come in any order (sorted by `at`).

- `SteamCurls`: wavy curls in paper rising from (x, y), the base of the steam
- `Splash`: a ring on the surface and drops thrown up, centred where the thing lands (x, y); drops filled with paper (or `color`)
- `SpeedLines`: strokes trailing behind a move; (x, y) is the moving thing, the strokes start behind it
- `SweatDrop`: a drop (`color`, default light blue) centred on (x, y), its tip up
- `PopMark`: "!" or "?" filled with spark (or `color`), (x, y) its centre
- `Cracks`: lines spreading from the hit point (x, y), kept inside `clip`
- `DustPuff`: two clouds (`color`, default light sand) rolling out sideways from (x, y) on the ground

**Drawn marks** (siblings of the effects: LineStyle ink, `at` and `len` in frames, nothing before `at`, a `seed`):
- By a head (`x, y, at, len, size, color`): `AngerVein` (pops in, throbs every `beat` frames), `Sparkles` (twinkle
  within `r`), `Hearts` (one rises every `every` frames), `Zzz`, `Tears` (`kind` "stream" or "drops", eyes `gap`
  apart), `GloomLines` (a small cloud, lines dropping over the head), `ShockLines` (ticks over the top `arc`
  degrees), `DizzyStars` (orbiting above the head), `IdeaBulb` (lights `click` frames after it pops on, then rays),
  `MusicNotes`. A question mark is `PopMark kind="?"`.
- Over an area (`x, y, w, h`): `Rain` (`slant`, `splash`), `Snow`, `Confetti` (`colors`), `Petals` (`kind` "petal"
  or "leaf"), `Bubbles` (pop at their own height). Already falling at `at`, they fade in over 6 frames and out at
  the end of `len` (default the whole clip).
- Impact: `Poof` (a puff that swells and breaks into curls; hide or show the thing at about 40 % of `len`),
  `ImpactFlash` (a 1 to 3 frame impact frame over an area, ink wedges from `cx, cy`), `FocusLines` (concentration
  lines round the area's edge toward `cx, cy`, clear inside `inner`, redrawn on twos).
- Furniture: `SpeechBubble` (`shape` "round" or "box") and `ThoughtBubble`, centred on (x, y), `w` × `h`, a tail
  toward `tail` [x, y]; they pop in with a squash, pop out over the last 5 frames of `len`, and the children are the
  words. `ChalkBoard` draws a framed board at (x, y, w, h) (`stand` adds legs); inside it `ChalkText` (written on
  by `p` or by `at`/`len`, lines split on "\n"), `ChalkLine` (`from`, `to`) and `ChalkPath` (`d`).
- Pure helpers for your own marks: `popAt`, `spawnsAt`, `fallAt`, `riseAt`, `focusLinesAt`, `writeAt`, `chalkLine`.

```tsx
<AngerVein x={hx + 90} y={hy - 60} at={T("furious")} />
<SpeechBubble x={540} y={560} w={600} h={280} tail={[hx, hy]} at={T("why")} len={60}>Why?</SpeechBubble>
<FocusLines x={0} y={0} w={1080} h={1920} cx={hx} cy={hy} at={T("now")} len={20} />
```

**Craft helpers**:
- `Walker`: the two-drawing walk in and out. `<Walker drawings={[a.walk_a_asset, a.walk_b_asset]} at={T("enter")}
  x0={-200} x1={640} y={1600} h={1250} />` alternates the drawings every `step` (6) frames at an even pace, rises
  mid-step and squashes as a foot lands, stands on its soles as the Performer does (`feet`, `h`) and mirrors itself
  walking left (draw the steps facing right; `flip` overrides). `dur` defaults to whole strides of `stride`
  (0.28 h) px a drawing: match it to the drawing's step or the feet slide. `walkAt` gives the state at a frame.
- `Smear`: a smear frame instead of motion blur. `<Smear pos={(k) => [x(k), y(k)]} r={70}>...</Smear>` stretches
  its children along the move on frames faster than `fast` (28 px a frame), up to `max` (1.2: 2.2 times as long),
  about the leading edge `r` px ahead of the centre, with `lines` (3) ink lines trailing; `smearAt` gives it.
- `Brush`: a brush-pen stroke along points or a path `d`, filled in the LineStyle colour, tapered at the ends
  (`taper`, `thinning`, `size` default 1.6 times the line weight), drawn on by `p` (perfect-freehand).
- `Morph`: one closed shape turning into another, `<Morph from={puddle} to={splash} p={spr(f, T("hit"))}
  fill="#9FD8F2" />` (flubber; exact at p 0 and 1); `morphAt` gives the path.
- `Boil`: the lines of what it wraps boil, a displacement of `amount` (3) px re-seeded every `every` (2) frames
  through `drawings` (3) seeds. `box` [x, y, w, h] limits the filter (cheaper); nothing outside it is drawn.
  `boil` (in Time) instead jitters a whole object.
- `ramp(light, dark, steps)`: even OKLCH steps from one colour to another, both ends exact, for one shadow tone
  across props (`shades` stays the material recipe).
- `useBaked(data, f?)`: frame `f` of a simulation baked to JSON, held at both ends. Never step physics in a
  component (frames render in any order): `add_package(p, ["matter-js"])`, a script run once with
  `run_script(p, "library/tools/fall.mjs")` that steps the engine at 1000/30 ms and writes one `{x, y, a}` a frame
  to `${process.env.REELSMITH_LIBRARY_MADE}/fall.json`, then `import fall from "../made/fall.json"` and
  `const s = useBaked(fall, f - T("drop"))`.
- Other helpers: `sampleLine`, `partOf` (even points along a line, the first `p` of it), `brushPressure`, `boilSeed`.

**What reads as animation**: a new pose or action at least every 1.2 s of narration; anticipation before every move
(give `dir`); arcs and gravity for props, a squash where they land; the pose 2 or 3 frames before its word; the face
changes with the line; a close shot for a detail; never a picture slid to fake a camera; nothing held dead.

- **Acting inside a hold**: a hold over 1.2 s needs something: a nod or a tilt through `nudge` on each stressed word
  (`wob` as in the example), the same pose mirrored with `flip`, a second look through a twin.
- **Prop hand-off**: a prop that moves as its own cut-out is not also drawn in the pose at that moment. On the frame
  it leaves the hand, switch to a pose or twin with the hand empty, and start the cut-out from the hand's position.
- **Judge it on sheets**: pacing on a 6-a-second sheet, `frames(p, "build/draft.mp4", every=0.1667, start=2.4,
  end=7.2)`; each pose change on a 25-a-second sheet of the half second around it, `frames(p, "build/draft.mp4",
  every=0.04, start=3.9, end=4.4)` (0.04 is the smallest `every`): the crouch, the snap and the settle must be visible.

**On model**: the sheet as `style_ref_asset` every time, the outfit named every time, each new pose looked at beside
the sheet and on a PoseSheet with the others.
**Limits**: limbs jump between drawings (more poses smooth it, not tweening); two or three mouth shapes, by
loudness only.

### Stock: poses, sets and props that stay

A workspace keeps reusable pictures in `library/stock` (`poses/`, `sets/`, `props/`: each item its pictures and an
`<id>.json`), all in one look set once in `library/stock/look.json`: `{"character": "<style sentence>", "set":
"<style sentence>", "prop": "<style sentence>", "sheet": "<optional: a picture in library/stock that shows the look,
sent as the style reference>", "who": "<optional: the workspace's one fixed character and its outfit, as every pose
names it; sheet is then its model sheet>"}`. A shipped catalogue names standard items: 54 poses (expressions,
gestures, actions and the bridge poses `crouch`, `lean_back`, `turn`), 38 sets and 66 props.

- `stock(p)` lists what is kept, then the catalogue ids not yet kept; `kind` lists one kind with what each is;
  `name="pose:shrug"` shows one item in full, or the draws `take` would make for it.
- `take(p, items)` copies kept items into `assets/`; a catalogue item not yet kept is drawn first in the look (a
  pose: base, closed-mouth twin, blink, 3 draws; a set: the plate, then each piece matched to it; a prop: one
  cut-out) and kept for every later film. Without a complete `look.json` it takes kept items only.
- `take(p, ["pose:shrug", "pose:shock"], cast="granny", who="an old woman, grey bun, floral padded jacket")` casts a
  character for this film alone: the model sheet `granny_sheet` is drawn first in the look (or is the one you
  drew under that id), then each catalogue pose from it as `granny_shrug` with its twins. Cast poses stay in the
  project and are not kept. Take one pose first, look at the sheet and the pose, then take the rest with the same
  `who`. A pose the catalogue lacks: `draw` it with `style_ref_asset="granny_sheet"`. A workspace whose
  `look.json` has no `who` has no fixed character: its catalogue poses are drawn only this way.
- `keep(p, ids, kind)` saves the project's own drawings: a pose with its twins found by name, a set's layers back
  to front, props one per id; an item already kept needs `replace=true`.

```text
take(p, ["pose:shrug", "set:kitchen", "prop:coin"])
→ assets shrug, shrug_shut, shrug_blink; kitchen_plate, kitchen_counter; coin
For the component:
const poses = { shrug: { src: a.shrug_asset, shut: a.shrug_shut_asset, blink: a.shrug_blink_asset, feet: 0.50 } };
set kitchen back to front: kitchen_plate, the figure, kitchen_counter (...)
clip props: "pics": {"shrug_asset": "shrug", ...}
```

Paste the `poses` line and the `pics` map; draw the plate, the Performer, then the front pieces. Cut-outs come back
at their own size: scale props and front pieces to the plate and the figure yourself.

### Places drawn in code

A set component draws a whole place in code, every plane its own drawing, so a camera may travel through it. In
a drawn-pose film the framing changes by hard cuts (`Framing`) instead. Place first, then the stops, then perform:

1. **Place.** Pick a set and give it a `span` wide enough for every station (`[-0.3 * w, 2.9 * w]` for four
   stops). The set paints the far planes, the surface and the foreground; `useSet().floor` is where things stand.
2. **Camera stops.** One stop per idea, each arriving a few frames before its word:
   `cam={stations([{ at: 0, x, y, z: 1.15 }, { at: T("sea") + 4, x, y }], { until: dur })}`. Between stops the
   camera travels with an eased move and a small zoom dip; at a stop it keeps pushing in slowly.
3. **Objects with material.** Draw each story object as your own path or shapes inside `Solid` (or `Gloss`,
   `Shadow`), never as a flat fill: a lit body, a rim, a darker underside and a contact shadow make it a thing.
4. **Perform on the word.** Things fall (`drop`), swing (`pendulum`), trail (`follow`), shake on impact (`shake`)
   and idle (`breathe`); nothing slides in and stops dead.

What makes a film look cheap:

- flat icons on a bare ground, no place around them;
- the picture only in the upper half, the lower third empty;
- objects that slide in and stop, with a locked-off camera;
- one object repeated unchanged (vary size, tone, seed and pose).

Every primitive is absolutely positioned in clip px and takes the frame from `useCurrentFrame()` unless given
`f`. Sizes left out scale with `useUnit()` (clip width / 1080; `72u` means `72 * useUnit()`); colours come from props
or `usePalette()`; randomness is seeded (the same `seed` draws the same tear on every frame); filter ids are per
instance. Each component's props type is exported as `<Name>Props`; `Actor`'s entrance and exit names are the types
`Entrance` and `Exit`.

### Time

- `useCues`: `cues(useClip().lines, fps)`: the narration cues of the current clip
- `cues`: `{T, at, end, S, lines, fps}` over `ClipLine[]` (type alias `CueLine`; result type `Cues`)
- `T(word, off = 0, nth = 0)`: the frame the `nth` occurrence of `word` is said (counted from 0, across the clip's lines), plus `off` seconds: `T("egg", 0, 1)` is the second "egg", `T("egg", -0.1)` a tenth of a second before the first. Placed by character position: every character of the line, punctuation included, takes an equal share of its time. Throws when the word (or that occurrence) is not in the lines
- `at`, `end`: first and last frame of line `i` (its `line:N` number), or of the clip's first / last line; throw when missing
- `S`: seconds to frames
- `prog`: 0..1 over `len` frames from `a`, held at both ends
- `clamp01`, `lerp`
- `out3`, `in2`, `inOut`, `backOut`: easings on 0..1 (`backOut` overshoots)
- `spr`: a bouncy `springAt` from `at` (damping 11, stiffness 170, mass 0.9 unless `cfg` says otherwise)
- `wob`: a decaying shake after a hit
- `swing`: a damped pendulum in degrees, for things on a string
- `step`: the frame held at `rate` poses a second (stop motion)
- `boil`: `[dx, dy]` hand-made jitter that changes 12 times a second
- `win`: 0..1 envelope: in from `a`, held, out at `b`

`wob`, `swing`, `step` and `boil` assume 30 fps: pass `fps` from `useClip()` on other profiles.

### Geometry, palette and units

- `Pt`: `[x, y]`
- `torn`: a seeded torn-paper outline (SVG path) through a polygon
- `bbox`, `rect`, `circle`: bounds `{x, y, w, h}`; polygons
- `smooth`, `smoothClosed`: Catmull-Rom paths through points (open, closed)
- `blob`: an organic closed shape with a flat bottom
- `roughRect`: a slightly uneven rounded rectangle
- `fibre`: a wavy line
- `along`: the point at fraction `t` of a polyline's length
- `mixColor`, `seedInt`
- `usePalette`: tokens `ink paper kraft kraftDeep kraftLight red lemon accent wood woodDeep wall wallDeep shadow steel sky haze leaf` (type `Palette`); `accent` is the theme's, and a `<Stage palette>` overrides any of them. `PALETTE` holds the defaults, `PaletteContext` carries an override
- `useUnit`: clip width / 1080
- `shadowCss`: a CSS drop-shadow for an object `lift` (0..1) off the page
- `shades`: `{base, hi, deep, rim}`
- `useFilters`: `{id, soft, blur, defs}`: per-instance soft-edge and blur filters

### Stage and grounds

- `Stage`: fills the clip: a ground (a node, painted on a depth-0.55 layer, or a CSS colour), the children, warm light from the top left, a vignette and faint grain. `drift` is a slow idle sway in px. A scene over a `ground` clip uses `grain={0} light={0} vignette={0}` and no ground, so it stays transparent
- `Layer`: a plane of the stage: under 1 lags behind the camera, over 1 runs ahead; parallax on x, y and zoom
- `Key`, `camAt`: camera keys `{at, x, y, z, dip?}` (frame, the point at the frame centre, zoom, a zoom-out mid-move); `camAt` gives `{x, y, z}` (type `Cam`)
- `useStage`: `{cam, w, h, f}` of the enclosing stage
- `Grain`: the grain alone; static unless `fps` > 0 (at most 6), `amount` at most 0.2
- `KraftGround`: brown paper with fibres and warm light; `pad` draws past the edges so camera moves never show a border
- `WallGround`: a wall of shelf bays and posts, an optional striped awning `[c1, c2]`, swaying lamps
- `TableTop`: fills the frame below `top` (default 0.62 h) with a table seen from the front, so the lower frame is never bare

### Sets

A set is a whole `Stage` (it takes every `Stage` prop: `cam`, `grain`, `light`, `vignette`, `drift`, `palette`)
with the place already built in depth: far planes that lag behind the camera, a surface at depth 1 that your
children stand on, blurred foreground pieces at the frame edges (`front`, default on) and some idle life. Children
are drawn on the action plane in clip px; do not wrap them in another `Layer`. Sizes scale with the clip (a
1080 x 1920 design, scaled by the tighter of width and height) and colours come from the palette.

- `SetProps`: shared by every set: `floor?` (y things stand on, clip px), `span = [-0.3w, 1.3w]` (x range the camera travels over at depth 1), `front = true`, `seed`, `back?` (drawn on the far plane: a sign on the wall, a plane in the sky), children (the action), plus every `Stage` prop
- `Room`: a back wall with windows, shelves and frames (0.8), pendant lamps (0.9), a plank floor with window light or a counter filling the lower frame; a leaf and a door jamb in front. Floor 0.66 h (counter 0.6 h)
- `Street`: clouds (0.15), a skyline (0.35), shopfronts with awnings and swinging signs (0.7), pavement, kerb and road (1); a lamp post in front. Floor 0.58 h; `useSet().near` is the road
- `Outdoors`: sky and sun (0.1), clouds (0.2), far hills or a skyline (0.35), near hills with swaying trees (0.6), a field, road or beach that moves in the wind (1). Floor 0.64 h; `sun` is a fraction of the frame or `null`
- `Desk`: a work surface from above under a warm lamp pool (0.85): papers, a pencil, a steaming mug and a plant round the edges with the centre clear; a blurred pencil and leaves in front (1.25)
- `useSet`: `{floor, near, x0, x1, w, h}` of the enclosing set (`SetInfo`)
- `Clouds`, `PendantLamp`, `Leaves`: the sets' idle pieces as SVG, for your own places

The far planes are static apart from their small idle pieces, so a set costs little to encode; only the camera
moves the whole frame.

### Materials

SVG wrappers that shade any shape you draw. Put them inside `<Canvas>` (an SVG over the whole clip in clip px
that never clips what is drawn past its edges) or inside your own `<svg>`. A shape is a path `d`, or SVG children
plus their `box`; only the children's silhouette counts, their own fills are ignored.

- `Canvas`: the SVG plane for materials and your own drawing
- `Box`: `[x, y, w, h]`
- `Solid`: a made thing: a contact shadow under the box (`shadow` strength, `lift` 0..1 off the surface), a darker thickness of `side` px, a lit radial body, a rim light along the lit edge, a darker underside, seeded specks, gloss and a faint ink line (paths only); `detail` is drawn on the body, clipped to it
- `Shadow`: a contact shadow: a dark core and a soft penumbra centred on the point under the object, spreading and fading with `lift`. Around children, `<Shadow lift>{...}</Shadow>` is a drop shadow of the children instead
- `Gloss`: a wet or polished highlight clipped to the shape; `sweep` 0..1 runs a glint band across it

### Motion

Pure functions of the frame; times in frames, 30 fps unless `fps` is given.

- `drop`: let go at `at`: `{y, sx, sy, t, landed}` (type `Drop`, options `DropOptions`). y accelerates from `from` to `to`, stretching a little; on landing it squashes (`sx` > 1, `sy` < 1) and settles. Scale around the contact point: `translate(cx, y) scale(sx, sy) translate(-cx, -to)`
- `pendulum`: degrees: a damped swing kicked at `at` (`swing`) that keeps an idle sway of `idle` degrees instead of stopping dead
- `follow`: a value trailing `leader` (a function of the frame) by about `lag` frames, smoothed: cargo, followers, a second hand
- `shake`: `[dx, dy]`: a smooth seeded shake from `at`, fading out over `dur` frames
- `breathe`: an idle scale around 1
- `stations`: camera keys for `<Stage cam>` (or a set's `cam`) from stops `{at, x, y, z = 1}` (type `Stop`, options `StationOptions`): the camera arrives at each stop's `at` after an eased `travel` with a zoom dip, pushes in by `push` while it holds, and keeps pushing on the last stop until `until`

### Actors

- `Placed`: `{poly, x, y, s?, r?}`: an outline in source pixels, its centre on the canvas, scale, rotation (deg)
- `Cut`: a torn cut-out of `poly` from a picture (an `*_asset` prop or a URL) with a paper keyline and a shadow by `lift`; `photo` is a CSS filter on the photo; `over` (clipped) and `free` draw on it in source pixels. Throws when the picture size is unknown: an asset ref carries it, a URL needs `imgW`/`imgH`
- `toCanvas`: where a source pixel of a placed piece lands on the canvas: pens, circles and loupes follow real detail with it
- `Loupe`: a round chip of the same picture enlarged around source point `at`, at canvas `(x, y)`; with `piece`, a leader line runs from the detail; `p` 0..1 pops it in
- `Magnifier`: a magnifying glass showing its children (canvas coordinates) enlarged
- `Actor`: wraps any child in an entrance (`drop left right up slap grow fade`), an idle boil and an exit (`left right up down shrink fade`); draws nothing before `at` or once it has left; `ox, oy` is the pivot

### Paper

- `Label`: a sticker sized to its text, centred on `(x, y)`; with `at` it slaps on at that frame; `peel` 0..1 peels it off
- `Tag`: a manila tag hanging from a pin at `(x, y)`, one big word or number (150 px at the default size) and a unit; `circle`, `tear`, `split` 0..1 loop it in red, crack it and pull it apart; sway it with `swing`
- `Stamp`: an ink stamp thumped down at `at`; the seed sets the ink texture
- `Tape`: a strip of masking tape
- `Card`: a stiff card that flips from word `a` to word `b` as `flip` goes 0 to 1

### Ink (`p` is 0..1 drawn)

- `Marker`: a felt-pen stroke along an SVG path in canvas px (red, 10u wide by default)
- `Highlighter`: a Marker 34u wide in lemon, multiplied at 0.8 opacity
- `Stroke`: a wide paint stroke kept inside the `clip` path
- `CircleIt`: a hand-drawn loop around a point
- `ArrowInk`: a drawn arrow between two points

### Effects

- `Mist`: a puff of spray leaving `(x, y)` `age` frames ago, travelling `dir` degrees (180 = left, 270 = up)
- `Wisps`: curls of steam or smell rising, `k` 0..1 how far
- `Drips`: drips running down from an edge
- `Sparks`: a burst of sparks

### Things

Drawn objects on one material recipe: a gradient body, a darker side, a rim light, a soft ground shadow and seeded
specks. All sizes have defaults relative to the clip width.

- `Slab`: any solid on a surface (a pile, a block, a stack); `(x, y)` is the centre of its top face
- `Hand`: a gloved hand on a sleeve; `(x, y)` is the fingertip (`point`), the pinch (`pinch`) or the palm (`open`); `ang` 0 points left, 90 up, 180 right, -90 down (type `HandPose`)
- `Wheel`: a wheel turned by `roll`
- `Sign`: a board hanging from a pin at `(x, y)`
- `Shelf`, `shelfBoards`: a shelf unit standing on `(x, y)`; `shelfBoards` gives the board tops to stand `items` on
- `Phone`: a phone; `screen` is an HTML node shown on it
- `Note`: a banknote of no real currency, `count` in a stack
- `Parcel`: a box; `(x, y)` is its front-bottom centre
- `Doc`: a document, its lines written in by `p`, an optional stamp
- `Car`: a car standing on `(x, y)`

### A complete scene

The last world of `examples/performance/` (`library/components/SaltDesk.tsx`) is a set-in-code scene: a `Desk`
set, three camera stops from `stations`, a taped `Cut` of a photo with a `Loupe` on its detail, a drawn dish and a
heap of salt made `Solid`, a `drop` with a squash and a `Tag` on a `pendulum`, every move cued with `T("word")`.

### Size and render cost

- Grain is static by default; per-frame grain over the whole picture multiplies the file size (keep `grainFps` ≤ 6).
- Aim for 8 Mb/s or less (a 60 s vertical film well under 80 MB). One set per world.
- A cut-out is one `<Img>` under a clip-path: cheap. Large blurred filters (`Mist` with a high `n`, a blurred
  `Layer`) cost the most render time; keep them to the moments that need them.
- Kit text is real DOM or SVG text, measured by the layout pass: keep it in the safe area and readable.

### Tracing a cut-out: AssetGrid

`reelsmith preview` cannot overlay a grid on an asset, so the built-in preset `AssetGrid` does: a component clip
with the full canvas box and `{"asset": "<image id>"}` (optional `cols`, `rows`, 2 to 40, default 10) shows the
picture whole on a dark ground under a labelled grid, every fifth line yellow, with the source image's pixel
coordinates on all four edges (fractions when the size is unknown). Preview it once per picture, read the outline's
points off it (10 to 14 points are enough), write them into the scene as `Pt[]` and remove the clip. It is a
tracing aid, not film content; its labels are not measured as film text.

### Photograph components

A reference for the components and tools that place and move generated photographs; `examples/layers/` uses them.
**Their limit**: a plate pushed or swayed, a cut-out slid across it, a clip baked from one still by its depth
(`parallax`) or frames interpolated between key pictures (`tween`) all move or warp one picture. Watched, that reads
as a warp, so none of them is a film's motion: make that with drawn-pose animation. `Photo`, `Surface`, `Vapor`,
`Glow`, `Lens` and the change of state are plain components that any world can use.

Pictures: a plate is an ordinary `draw` in the film's aspect; a cut-out is `draw(..., cutout=true)`, an isolated,
trimmed object with alpha (look at it over a coloured ground for a halo or a cropped edge). Trace fronts and lines
on an `AssetGrid` preview of the plate, in picture px, which `usePlate().at(pt)` (or `coverFit(...).at(pt)`) turns
into stage px.

- `Photo`: a picture standing with its `anchor` (fractions of the picture) on (x, y). `depth` gives it its own parallax layer; inside a `Layer` or `Plate` it takes theirs. Depth of field comes from the enclosing `Lens`; `smear` is motion blur in px along `smearAng`; `shade` darkens, `tint` at `tintA` multiplies in the plate's light, `haze` mixes toward the air; `sy` stretches it from the anchor. `shadow` 0..1 grounds it from its own alpha: a dark contact patch under its lowest pixels and, with a `light` (its own or the Plate's), a cast shadow laid away from the light that softens and fades with distance (`cast` its strength, `shadowW` the patch width; `ground` is the stage y of the floor under a thing in the air, or `lift` its height off the floor as a fraction of its drawn height). Both are built in stage space from the measured alpha, so rotation, `flip`, `sy` and empty rows at the bottom of a cut-out are honoured, and during a change (`to`, `p`) the two silhouettes' shadows cross-fade. `castMatrix` is the geometry; `useLight` the Plate's light
- **A change of state** (raw to cooked, off to on): both pictures from the same angle at the same size (a `to` whose aspect differs by over 1% is an error), then `<Photo src={a.raw} to={a.cooked} p={p} />`: a dissolve, or a `wipe` with a `soft` edge (`right left up down` is the direction it sweeps; `out` grows from `wipeAt`). `Plate` takes the same props; its fronts change with it
- `Surface`: children (text, a small component) on a quad traced in picture px (top-left, top-right, bottom-right, bottom-left); inside the `Photo`, `Travel` or `Plate` that holds the picture it follows every move in perspective and takes its blur. `ink="print"` multiplies the ink into the surface, `ink="glow"` is a lit screen. `usePicture()` (type `Picture`) gives the same mapping to your own children
- `Plate`: a full-frame picture on its own parallax layer, covering the frame with `pad` to spare; `drift` a slow move in px; `sway` (type `Sway`) `{y, amp = 1.2, hz = 0.35, feather = 60, gust?, gustAmp = 3}` bends the strip below picture row `y`. Children stand in it (stage px of its layer); `fronts` (polygons in picture px, `Pt[][]`) are drawn again over them with a soft edge. `light` (type `Light`, `{from: "upper-left", height: 0.5}`; `from` also `left right upper-right back front lower-left lower-right` or degrees) sets where the photos inside cast their shadows. `film` (type `Film`) plays a clip baked from the plate over the still from frame `film.at`, with `from`, `len` (seconds), `rate`, `loop` and `hold` (`"before"` returns to the still); a video `src` plays from frame 0. `punch` (type `Punch`, one or a list) is a hard cut from `at` (until `end`) to picture point `to`, `z` times closer (1.3 to 1.8), with its own `focus` and `aperture`; `punchAt`, `punchView` give the cue in force and its view
- `Footage`: a video clip standing like a `Photo`, timed by `at` (a frame), `rate`, `from`, `len` (seconds), `loop` (needs `len` or an asset ref) and `hold`; `still` is the picture it was made from, shown while it is not running, and gives the size (else `imgW`, `imgH`). A video has no alpha: a whole shot, a window or a screen, never a cut-out. `filmFrame` gives the clip frame at a frame; `FilmFrame` draws one; `isFilm` tells a video ref
- `Occluder`: one front placed by hand, in a `Layer` of the plate's depth with the plate's `pad` and `focus`
- `Travel`: a photo slid along `line` (stage px) from `at` for `dur`, with perspective `sizes`, a smear by speed, its shadow (`groundAt`), `shadeAt` and `turn`; `travelAt`, `pointAt` give its state; `shadeRamp` makes a `shadeAt` (0 at `from`, `max` at `to`)
- `pictureSize`, `Hold`, `SvgImage`: a picture's size from its ref; a hidden `<Img>` that holds the render until a picture has loaded; an SVG `<image>` that holds it until decoded (key it by `href`)
- Air and light: `Haze` (a band of air, clear at y0, thickest at y1), `Motes` (drifting dust, brighter near `light`), `Rays` (shafts from (x, y) at `ang`), `Glow` (a core and a soft tail; `squash` pooled on a surface, `flicker` 0.5 fire, 0.3 candle, 0 lamp), `Vapor` (steam or smoke rising from (x, y) `w` wide, spread by `wind`; `blend` `screen` for steam, `multiply` or `normal` with a grey `color` for smoke), `Embers` (sparks cooling from `hot` to `cool`)
- Liquid: `Droplet` (swells for `swell` s, falls at `at`, splats on `land`; `color`, `alpha`: water about `#DDEBF2` at 0.4, oil 0.75, syrup 0.9), `Pour` (a stream from the lip to `land` between `at` and `end`, curving with `vx`, a `pool` that stays or soaks in over `soak` s)
- Bending what it wraps: `Refract` (`heat` shimmer through the ellipse `area` [cx, cy, rx, ry]; `front` a ring of radius `r`; `ripple`; frame px), `Warp` (bulge `sx`/`sy`, `skew`, `rot` around `origin`, `dx`/`dy`), `Streak` (scenery past an opening; `pos` from `distanceAt(f, speed)`), `Pane` (glass: `dark` 0..1, `reflect` more as it darkens)
- `Lens`: the camera body around a world (the `Stage` inside sets `grain={0} light={0} vignette={0}`): depth of field (`aperture` px of blur per unit of depth from `focus`), one `grade` `{lift, gamma, gain, tint, tintA}`, static grain, impact `kick` `{at, amp = 22, dur = 14, fringe = 6}` and a hand-held drift. Types `Grade`, `Kick`, `LensState`
- `Join`: from scene `a` into `b` instead of a cut: `push` into `hole` [x, y, w, h] (frame px; `oval`), `whip` toward `dir`, `match` the shape `from` [x, y, size] onto `to`
- `onScreen`, `lookAt`, `joinAt`: where a stage point of a layer lands in the frame (and its scale), the camera x, y that centres it, a join's eased progress

Cost: plates, fronts and cut-outs are images under CSS transforms; an effect costs a second draw of what it wraps
(`Refract`, `Warp`, a `sway` strip) or a filter over its area (`Haze`, `Rays`, `Vapor`, the `Lens` grade, a shadowed
`Photo`), free when idle. Keep one or two active, around their words.

### The Broll preset

`Broll` plays a list of shots on a full-canvas component clip, each one plate and optionally a clip baked from it
(`film_asset`, made by `tween` or `parallax`), cutting hard at each shot's `at`. It eases a `pan`, `tilt` or `zoom`
across the plate with a hand-held wobble (`hand`), stops at the plate's edge, and draws `steam`, `air` (snow, dust)
and `movers` (cut-outs along a `line`) live. Every position and length in a shot is in the plate's own px: read them
off an `AssetGrid` preview of the plate, not off a Broll still. It moves and warps single pictures, with the limit
stated above.
